OUR STORY

Three decades on the Dinanagar route.

Bhawani Traders was built on a simple idea — a retailer should never have to chase their distributor. What started as a small counter operation on Railway Road grew, order by order, into one of the most dependable Hindustan Unilever channel partners in the region.

Today, we run a full warehousing and delivery operation for HUL's Personal Care, Home Care, Foods and Health & Hygiene ranges, serving retailers across Dinanagar and the surrounding Punjab markets with the same discipline we started with.
